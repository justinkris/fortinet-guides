  6.  Comparison drills for commonly confused exam concepts
  7.  NSE7 relevance flagging (use ⚑ NSE7 inline for exam-focus areas)

How to Use This Study Plan
1
Set up your Claude Project once

Create a Claude Project (not a regular chat). Copy the block above and paste it into the Project's Custom Instructions. This sets the persistent tutor context — you only do this once.

2
Pick a session and do the pre-reading (5–10 min)

Start at Session 01 and work sequentially. Click "Pre-Reading" to open the session's prep page. Read the opening story, skim the topic overview, and review the target questions so you know what you're aiming for. Write down any questions you want to bring to Claude.

3
Start the Claude session

Open your Claude Project. Click "Copy Prompt" on the session card below — the prompt is generated fresh with today's day count. Paste it into Claude and send. Claude will open with the story and follow the full session flow.

4
Work through the flow — let Claude lead

Don't skip steps. Let Claude ask the questions. Blurt everything you know in the recall phase. Accept corrections without defending wrong answers. The quiz at the end is honest feedback — treat it as a signal, not a grade.

5
Reflect and mark done

After the quiz, come back here and click "Mark Done". Go back to the pre-reading page and write your post-session notes while they're fresh. Then rest — or go straight to the next session if you have energy.
