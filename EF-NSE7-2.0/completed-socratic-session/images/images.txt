Shared style preamble (every image):
  Flat minimalistic cartoon illustration. Highly detailed, clean, and easy to understand.
  Modern vector-art aesthetic. Bold clean outlines. Warm and inviting colour palette.
  Cream/peach background (#fdf5f0). Coral accents (#e07050). Soft brown outlines (#7a5050).
  Muted gold (#d2a541). Sage green (#5ab288). No gradients. No photorealism. No 3D rendering.
