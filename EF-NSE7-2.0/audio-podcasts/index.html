NotebookLM Workflow
1
Open NotebookLM and create (or open) a notebook

You can keep one notebook per chapter, or one per episode. Optionally upload Fortinet documentation pages, the EF 7.6 study guide, or your own notes as sources for richer grounding.

2
Click "Audio Overview" → "Customize"

NotebookLM's customize box accepts a focus prompt that steers the two-host conversation. That's where each episode's prompt below goes.

3
Click "Copy Prompt" on the episode card

The copied prompt enumerates every subtopic, sets the audience (NSE7 candidate with prior FortiGate experience), tone, depth requirements, and ends with 3 exam-style recap questions.

4
Paste, generate, and advance the state pill

Click the state pill on the card to walk it through: Generating while NotebookLM works, Renamed once you've named the downloaded file, Completed when it's in your library.

5
Listen, then come back for the next episode

Sequential listening matches the dependency order baked into the prompts, but you can jump around — each episode is self-contained.
