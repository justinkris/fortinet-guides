Chapter banner image prompts — EF-NSE7 2.0 Socratic Sessions

Style brief (applies to every banner):
  Flat minimalistic detailed cartoon illustration. Wide banner (3:1 ratio, ~1200x400). Warm light palette
  matching the webpage: cream/peach background (#fdf5f0 to #fff8f5), coral accents (#e07050), soft brown
  outlines (#7a5050), occasional muted gold (#d2a541) and sage green (#5ab288) highlights. Clean line work,
  no gradients, no shadows beyond subtle ones. Generous whitespace. No text, no logos. Composition reads
  left-to-right as a single horizontal scene. Subjects sit on a faint horizon or floor line.
